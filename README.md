## 🌟 About Us

**The Braniacs** is a dynamic group of diverse individuals united towards shaping the future of the youth in Kenya. Our mission is to help young people tackle the challenge of job creation through meaningful interaction with art, design, and culture to promote community development.

### Our Mission

> "Our mission is to help the youth of Kenya tackle the challenge of job creation, through interaction with art, design, and culture to promote the community"

## 🎯 What We Do

We engage in real-world scenarios that foster innovative thinking and problem-solving skills. Through our E-Lab challenges, we immerse ourselves in complex situations that prepare us for making a real impact in our communities.

## 🚀 E-Lab Challenges

Our think tank has successfully completed six transformative challenges:

### Challenge 1: Elevator Pitch
We introduced ourselves, sharing our backgrounds, interests, contributions to the think tank, and learning goals from our E-Lab class.

### Challenge 2: Discover Africa
We explored the Maasai people, discussing fascinating facts, addressing misconceptions, identifying challenges they face, and celebrating their unique cultural heritage.

### Challenge 3: Help Lab
We put learning into action by helping build a house for a homeless lady whose home was destroyed by heavy rain, demonstrating our commitment to community impact.

### Challenge 4: Hunt for Treasure
We interviewed a small business owner about their entrepreneurial journey, learning about the process of starting a business, current operations, and their mission.

### Challenge 5: Launch Your Mission
We pitched our mission, presented the problem we identified through research, explained our project idea, demonstrated how it addresses the problem, and competed for a 10k grant.

### Challenge 6: Digital Print
We created this website to establish our digital presence and showcase our work and mission to the world.

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Viewing the Website

1. **Clone the repository:**
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/Masalale/the_braniacs.git
   cd the_braniacs
   ```

2. **Open in browser:**
   - Simply open `index.html` in your web browser
   - Or use a local server:
     ```bash
     # Using Python 3
     python -m http.server 8000
     
     # Using Python 2
     python -m SimpleHTTPServer 8000
     
     # Using Node.js
     npx http-server
     ```

3. **Navigate to:**
   - `http://localhost:8000` (or the port you specified)
